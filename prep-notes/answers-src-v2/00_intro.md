## A0.1 How to navigate (works in Word and in the PDF)

- Click any line in the **Contents** to jump to that category, sub-category or question.
- At the end of every question, use **Back to Contents** or **Back to category** to return.
- In the appendix, the source-mapping table links each question number from your Top 70 document to its place in this book.
- If you convert this file to PDF yourself, use Word's Save As PDF with the option to create bookmarks from headings, so the links and the bookmark panel are kept.

## A0.2 Answer conventions

- **Experience and behavioral answers** use the STAR flow written as a natural spoken paragraph. Each carries a tag:
  - **Real story:** built from a project in your resume, case-study notes or attached document, with your documented numbers.
  - **Approach answer:** you have no documented instance, so the answer explains how you would act and borrows only real, related evidence. Replace it with a true instance if you have one.
- **Case and design answers** state clarifying questions, assumptions and a named framework, then give the structured analysis and a recommendation.
- **Estimates** show assumptions, arithmetic and a sanity check. Figures marked illustrative are made-up planning numbers to demonstrate method, not Optum data.
- **Your career numbers** are as in your resume and attached document. Optum figures are company-reported. Re-check dates and regulatory points before quoting.
- **Client names** are replaced with generic descriptions because of your NDA. Employer names (o9, Publicis Sapient, Reliance Industries, Ninjacart) are fine to say.
- **Tools:** you are proficient in Aha!, Asana, Power BI and Tableau but have not used them recently, so the answers say you would be back at full speed within a few weeks. Rally is not claimed.
