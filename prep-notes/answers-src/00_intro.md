# A0. How to Use These Answers

> What this is: model answers to all 270 questions in the Top 10 Interview Questions document, in the same order and numbering. Questions are numbered Q1.1.1 (category, sub-category, question). Answers are written to be spoken or adapted, not memorized.

## A0.1 Answer conventions

- **Experience and Behavioral questions** use the STAR flow (situation, task, action, result) written as a natural spoken paragraph. Each is tagged:
  - **Real story:** built from a project in your resume or case-study notes, with your documented numbers. Client names stay out because of your NDA.
  - **Approach answer:** you have no documented instance, so the answer explains how you would act and borrows only real, related evidence. If you have a true instance, replace it.
- **Case and design questions** follow one pattern: clarifying questions, stated assumptions, a named framework, the structured analysis, and a closing recommendation.
- **Estimates** show assumptions, arithmetic and a sanity check. All figures marked illustrative are made-up planning numbers to demonstrate method, not Optum data.
- **Numbers from your career** are labeled achieved, pilot, targeted or estimated as in the story bank. Optum figures are company-reported.
- **Dates and rules** (for example CMS timelines) are from secondary sources. Re-verify before quoting.

## A0.2 Frameworks used in the answers

| Family | Frameworks | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| Product management case | CIRCLES (comprehend, identify, report, cut, list, evaluate, summarize); problem to metrics flow (goal, users, pain, solutions, prioritize, metrics); issue trees (MECE); RICE and WSJF; Kano; North Star and input-metric trees; jobs-to-be-done; Rumelt strategy kernel (diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions); three horizons | Strategy, roadmap, prioritization, discovery, metrics |
| AI product | Use-case fit test (task, data, risk, value); layered RAG pipeline; evaluation pyramid (unit, component, end-to-end, online); autonomy ladder; risk tiering aligned to NIST AI RMF (govern, map, measure, manage); build, buy or partner; unit economics per task | Gen AI, agents, ML, governance |
| System design | R-C-A-D-T: requirements, capacity estimate, APIs and data, design components, trade-offs and risks | Platform and architecture design questions |
| Technical | Architecture decision records; CAP and PACELC; Well-Architected pillars (reliability, security, cost, performance, operations); DORA metrics; SLI, SLO and error budgets; STRIDE threat modeling; zero trust; FinOps | Architecture, cloud, data, DevOps, security |
