# Q3. Product Management: Top 10 Questions per Sub-category

## Q3.1 Strategy, vision and roadmap

- **1. [Conceptual]** What makes a product strategy good rather than just a list of features?
  - Look for: diagnosis, choices, what you will not do, measurable outcomes.
- **2. [Experience]** Walk us through a multi-year strategy you defined. How did you win leadership alignment?
  - Look for: customer problem, options, investment case, stakeholder management.
- **3. [Technical]** How would you build a roadmap for a payment integrity platform serving several lines of business?
  - Look for: themes, sequencing by value and readiness, compliance dates, platform health.
- **4. [Conceptual]** How do you balance platform stability, new capabilities and incremental workflow improvements?
  - Look for: capacity allocation and explicit trade-offs.
- **5. [Behavioral]** Leadership asks you to change the roadmap mid-quarter. How do you respond?
  - Look for: impact analysis, options, decision log, communication.
- **6. [Experience]** Tell us about a bet you made that did not pay off. What did you learn?
  - Look for: accountability and learning, not blame.
- **7. [Conceptual]** How do you create a roadmap that is transparent without over-committing?
  - Look for: horizons, confidence levels, outcome focus.
- **8. [Experience]** How have you managed a portfolio and killed or merged low-value work?
  - Look for: criteria, data, stakeholder handling.
- **9. [Conceptual]** How would you align a US-India product organisation on one strategy?
  - Look for: shared goals, clear decision rights, rhythm of forums.
- **10. [Behavioral]** Describe how you foster innovation while delivering committed work.
  - Look for: time-boxed experiments, stage gates, protected capacity.

## Q3.2 Requirements and backlog

- **1. [Technical]** Take a complex capability, such as a rules engine for claim edits, and show how you would decompose it into epics and stories.
  - Look for: vertical slicing, enablers, acceptance criteria, NFRs.
- **2. [Conceptual]** What belongs in a PRD for a regulated platform?
  - Look for: problem, users, scope, non-goals, metrics, compliance, rollout.
- **3. [Experience]** How do you ensure the backlog is ready before PI or sprint planning?
  - Look for: refinement cadence, ready criteria, dependency checks.
- **4. [Technical]** How do you write good acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements?
  - Look for: testable conditions, quantified performance, security and audit.
- **5. [Conceptual]** What is the role of enablers and technical debt items in the backlog?
  - Look for: making value visible, protected capacity.
- **6. [Experience]** Tell us about a time unclear requirements caused rework. What changed afterward?
  - Look for: root cause and process improvement.
- **7. [Behavioral]** Engineers say your stories are too vague. How do you respond?
  - Look for: openness, co-writing, definition of ready.
- **8. [Technical]** How do you maintain traceability from initiative to feature to story to release?
  - Look for: tool integration, IDs, reporting.
- **9. [Conceptual]** What does a rigorous Definition of Done include beyond functional completeness?
  - Look for: performance, security, operational readiness, documentation.
- **10. [Experience]** How do you translate business rules from operations into engineering artifacts?
  - Look for: examples, decision tables, validation with real cases.

## Q3.3 Prioritization and decision-making

- **1. [Conceptual]** Which prioritisation frameworks do you use and when do they mislead?
  - Look for: RICE, WSJF, cost of delay; judgement beyond scores.
- **2. [Experience]** Describe your hardest prioritisation decision. What did you choose and why?
  - Look for: criteria, data, stakeholders, outcome.
- **3. [Technical]** How do you build a business case for a multi-year platform investment?
  - Look for: options, benefits, costs including run costs, sensitivity.
- **4. [Behavioral]** Two senior stakeholders demand conflicting priorities. What do you do?
  - Look for: facilitation, transparent criteria, escalation with recommendation.
- **5. [Conceptual]** How do you decide with incomplete information?
  - Look for: reversible versus irreversible decisions, experiments.
- **6. [Experience]** Tell us about a time data contradicted your intuition.
  - Look for: humility and action.
- **7. [Conceptual]** How do you treat regulatory deadlines versus value-based prioritisation?
  - Look for: cost of delay, fixed-date items, buffers.
- **8. [Experience]** How have you used win/loss analysis to shape a roadmap?
  - Look for: structured interviews and decisions taken.
- **9. [Behavioral]** Describe saying no to an important stakeholder.
  - Look for: respectful reasoning and alternatives.
- **10. [Technical]** How do you quantify trade-offs between customer value, engineering effort and security risk?
  - Look for: decision documents, risk scoring, explicit assumptions.

## Q3.4 Discovery, customer insight and market intelligence

- **1. [Experience]** How do you run continuous discovery for internal operational users?
  - Look for: regular touchpoints, shadowing, real work samples.
- **2. [Conceptual]** How do you combine qualitative and quantitative feedback into themes?
  - Look for: triangulation across sources.
- **3. [Experience]** Describe a Voice of the Customer programme you ran or improved.
  - Look for: sources, cadence, closed loop, impact.
- **4. [Technical]** How would you use AI to summarise customer feedback while keeping it trustworthy?
  - Look for: traceability, sampling, human validation.
- **5. [Conceptual]** What is jobs-to-be-done and how has it shaped your work?
  - Look for: practical example.
- **6. [Experience]** Tell us about a competitor move that changed your plans.
  - Look for: analysis and measured response.
- **7. [Conceptual]** How do you detect early signals of defects or friction and route them?
  - Look for: thresholds, ownership, response times.
- **8. [Behavioral]** Customers ask for a feature that conflicts with your strategy. What do you do?
  - Look for: understanding the underlying need.
- **9. [Experience]** How have you benchmarked and improved a business process before building technology?
  - Look for: baselines, value stream mapping.
- **10. [Conceptual]** How would you assess market direction for payment integrity and RCM?
  - Look for: regulation, AI, consolidation, payer-provider dynamics.

## Q3.5 Product lifecycle and launch

- **1. [Experience]** Walk us through an end-to-end product launch you led and its adoption results.
  - Look for: enablement, rollout, metrics.
- **2. [Conceptual]** What are your exit criteria for Alpha, Beta and general availability?
  - Look for: quality, support, security, commercial readiness.
- **3. [Experience]** How did you plan a multi-wave customer deployment?
  - Look for: sequencing, shared playbooks, feedback loop.
- **4. [Conceptual]** When and how should you retire a feature or product?
  - Look for: criteria, migration, communication.
- **5. [Behavioral]** Adoption is low after launch. What do you do?
  - Look for: diagnosis, user conversations, instrumentation, fixes.
- **6. [Technical]** What belongs in an operational readiness checklist?
  - Look for: runbooks, monitoring, training, incident process.
- **7. [Experience]** Describe a launch that went badly. What was your role in recovery?
  - Look for: ownership, transparency.
- **8. [Conceptual]** How do you use feature flags and phased rollouts for AI features?
  - Look for: risk control and learning.
- **9. [Behavioral]** Sales or operations want to launch before you consider it ready. What do you do?
  - Look for: criteria-based decision, risk communication.
- **10. [Experience]** How do you run effective demos for stakeholders and customers?
  - Look for: outcome focus, real data, captured actions.

- **4. [Technical]** How do you measure the success of a developer or AI platform?

- **7. [Technical]** How would you use DORA metrics to discuss delivery predictability?

- **8. [Behavioral]** A KPI is declining. How do you present it to leadership?
  - Look for: candour, root cause, plan.
- **9. [Experience]** Describe dashboards or scorecards you built and who used them.
  - Look for: decision usefulness.
- **10. [Conceptual]** How do you use experiments or holdouts to prove impact?
  - Look for: design and limitations.

## Q3.7 Platform and technical product management

- **3. [Experience]** How did you drive adoption of a platform that teams were not required to use?

- **7. [Behavioral]** A large internal customer demands a custom feature. What do you do?
  - Look for: generalising the need and saying no.
- **8. [Technical]** How do you reduce time to onboard a new consumer onto the platform?
  - Look for: self-service, templates, documentation.
- **9. [Conceptual]** How do you balance reliability work with new features?
  - Look for: error budgets and capacity allocation.
- **10. [Experience]** How have you coordinated delivery across multiple engineering teams?
  - Look for: dependency mapping, PI-style planning, escalation.

# Q4. Technical Expertise: Top 10 Questions per Sub-category

- **3. [Technical]** How would you integrate a modern product with a legacy claims core?
  - Look for: anti-corruption layer, phased migration, risk management.
- **4. [Conceptual]** What does CAP theorem mean for a real-time eligibility service?
  - Look for: consistency versus availability under partition and business impact.
- **5. [Experience]** Tell us about a technical trade-off you influenced between engineering and the business.
  - Look for: options, consequences, decision.
- **6. [Behavioral]** An architect disagrees with your scope. How do you reach alignment?
  - Look for: respect, evidence, shared goals.
- **7. [Conceptual]** How do you handle technical debt in planning?
  - Look for: register, capacity share, impact framing.
- **8. [Technical]** How do you specify non-functional requirements for a high-volume claims service?
  - Look for: latency, throughput, availability, recovery targets.
- **9. [Conceptual]** How does multi-tenancy affect product design?
  - Look for: data isolation, configuration, noisy neighbours.
- **10. [Experience]** Describe how you got credibility with a highly technical engineering team.
  - Look for: preparation, curiosity, follow-through.
