## A1. The 60-second snapshot

- **UnitedHealth Group (UHG)** runs two platforms: **UnitedHealthcare** (insurance and benefits) and **Optum** (health services, data, technology and pharmacy). It reports four segments: UnitedHealthcare, Optum Health, Optum Insight, Optum Rx.
  - Q3 2026 (reported 13 Oct 2026): revenue about $112.7B; adjusted EPS $4.69 (vs $2.92); medical care ratio 88.4%; FY2026 adjusted EPS guided to at least $20.50.
  - Optum Q3 2026: revenue $66.9B, operating earnings $3.7B (5.6% margin); about 120M patients and consumers served.
  - UnitedHealthcare Q3 2026: revenue $86.3B; 48.3M consumers served.
- **The 2026 story is a repair-and-reinvest year.** Margins are being rebuilt, unprofitable footprints are being shrunk (Optum Health value-based patients fell; Medicaid is called the trough year; remaining South American operations are being exited), and AI is positioned as both a productivity lever and a new external product line.
- **Leadership names worth knowing** (from Q3 2026 earnings coverage):
  - Stephen Hemsley: Chairman and CEO of UHG.
  - Patrick Conway: CEO of Optum.
  - Wayne DeVeydt: CFO.
  - Sandeep Dadlani: leads Optum Insight (described as CEO of Optum Insight on the Q3 call; one trade source calls him UHG's Chief Digital and Technology Officer, so verify the title before you use it).
  - Tim Noel: leads UnitedHealthcare.
  - Puneet Maheshwari: General Manager, Optum Real.
  - Rahul Bhotika: reported as Chief AI Officer (verify).
- **Optum Global Solutions / Optum India** is UHG's global capability centre. Your role sits inside the engine that builds and runs many of these products.

### Optum Health (product lens)
- **Strategy shift:** contract and tighten the network, prioritise high-performing engaged providers, move toward employed or contractually dedicated physicians. Management expects further contraction before margin recovery (6–8% target by 2028).
- **Scale signals:** the Q3 2026 call cited about 20M patients with about 4M in fully accountable (full-risk) relationships. Older coverage cited more than 5M in full-risk and 7M in two-sided-risk models, so quote the most recent figure and the date (verify).
- **AI in the workflow (company-reported):** AI chart summarisation reaches over 40% of employed clinicians and cut chart review time by 33%; ambient documentation and next-best-action prompts; online self-scheduling growth.
- **Value Connect (launched February 2026):** an AI-first, cloud-native platform for payers and providers in value-based care. It combines clinical, operational and financial data, uses more than 1,600 models (including generative AI), and routes interventions into care-team workflows. Early-adopter claims: 35% lower medical spend, 17% lower pharmacy cost, 20% higher care-gap closure (company-reported; Keck Medicine of USC cited). It is paired with **Value Enablement Services**, a hands-on services layer.
- **Why this matters for you:** it shows Optum's pattern of selling **platform plus services**. Expect interview questions on when to productise a service and when to keep humans in the loop.

### Optum Rx (product lens)
- **Pricing model change:** May 2026 announcement of a transparent pharmacy care model with clearly defined monthly per-member fees, independent of manufacturer list prices or prescription volume. The Q2 2026 filing says rebate discounts will be passed through 100% to clients by 1 January 2028; another source says the full transition to flat fees will complete by the end of 2027 (verify which date to quote).
- **PreCheck Prior Authorization:** integrates with the EHR via the Surescripts network to check criteria at the point of prescribing. Company-reported: approval time from over 8 hours to under 30 seconds, 68% fewer denials due to missing information, 88% fewer appeals. Cleveland Clinic partnership cited (100% accuracy after one year, 67% automation rate).
- **Reauthorisation reduction:** 33% of drug reauthorisations eliminated, equal to 11% of pharmacy prior authorizations (company-reported).
- **Consumer tool:** Shop MyScript shows price and pharmacy options right after a prescription is written.
- **Context:** PBMs face antitrust and legislative scrutiny. Acknowledge this when you discuss transparency; do not oversell it.

### Optum Insight (product lens, closest to your role)
- **What it is:** the technology and data business serving payers, providers and governments. It inherited the Change Healthcare assets (claims, eligibility, ePA and the Intelligent Healthcare Network), owns InterQual (clinical criteria used in medical necessity decisions), and has payment integrity and revenue cycle portfolios.
- **Strategic posture (Q3 2026 call):** rationalise existing products and contracts, infuse AI into current offerings, and launch AI-first products and platforms. A series of launches was promised "over the next two quarters".
- **Return expectations:** Dadlani was quoted expecting a conservative 2:1 return on AI programmes over the next few years, with many paying back within 12–18 months.
- **Recognition:** named a Leader in Everest Group's RCM Platforms PEAK Matrix (2026) and described as the highest-designated Leader in payer payment integrity in an earlier PEAK assessment (date unclear).

## A4. AI strategy, partnerships, governance and risk

- **Investment and posture:** about $1.5B on AI reported for 2026 (verify; a blog headline of "$3B" is not corroborated). Operating cost ratio rose partly because of technology and AI investment. AI is framed as productivity (claims, prior authorization, documentation, appeals) and as products sold externally.
- **Evidence points management cites (company-reported):** chart review time down 33%; appeals cycle time down 26%; complex Medicaid claims adjudicated "in seconds instead of days" using agentic AI; prior authorization volume targeted down 30% by end 2026; AI plan recommendations for the Medicare Advantage enrollment period.
- **Partnerships:** Optum Insight has go-to-market partnerships with Microsoft and Google and, per the Q3 2026 call, joined the **Anthropic Partner Network**. One secondary blog claims a broader July 2026 deployment of Claude across Optum operations; that is not corroborated (verify before citing).
- **Governance:** UHG states it has a formal Responsible AI programme across UnitedHealthcare and Optum. Secondary sources describe an AI review board and model governance approvals, and job postings reference an AI Review Board for appeals AI. Principles stressed: fairness, transparency, safety, clinicians in the loop. No official public description of the model approval workflow was found.
- **Risks to be fluent about:**
  - **nH Predict litigation:** a lawsuit alleging an algorithm drove inappropriate coverage denials; reports say a Minnesota federal court ordered document production in March 2026 (secondary source). It shapes how Optum talks about "no automated denials" and human oversight.
  - **Change Healthcare cyberattack (February 2024):** a ransomware incident that disrupted claims flows industry-wide (widely reported; general knowledge, not from this research round). Resilience, security and vendor concentration are live concerns.
  - **Scrutiny of vertical integration:** Optum owning payer, provider and platform raises conflict-of-interest questions about vendor-reported results (for example Optum Real metrics).
  - **Regulation:** CMS prior authorization rule timelines (see Healthcare Domain notes), state rules on AI in utilisation review, No Surprises Act arbitration costs (cited by management as a commercial cost trend driver).

## A6. Positioning yourself as Lead Technical PM (Associate Director)

- **Which JD is this?** The closest match in your set is the Lead Technical Product Manager role for payment integrity, audit and recovery (13+ years, leads product managers, enterprise platform, claims workflows). Confirm the exact requisition, and ask what "CAP" stands for in that posting; the JD does not define it.
- **The narrative to land:** "I turn complex, regulated workflows into configurable platforms, with measurable outcomes (yield, cycle time, adoption), using AI where it earns trust and humans where it must."
- **Associate Director signals interviewers look for:**
  - Owning a multi-year strategy and portfolio, not a backlog.
  - Leading and growing product managers (coaching, hiring, goals, career paths).
  - Making trade-offs between platform stability, new capability and workflow polish.
  - Executive narrative: outcomes, investment cases, risk framing.
  - Credibility with engineering and architecture; comfort with APIs, data flows, cloud and AI runtimes.
- **Six STAR stories to prepare:**
  1. A platform you built or reshaped to serve multiple clients or lines of business.
  2. A measurable outcome you moved (cost, cycle time, yield, adoption), with the baseline and method.
  3. An AI or automation feature with a human-in-the-loop design and an evaluation approach.
  4. Influencing without authority across engineering, compliance and operations.
  5. A hard prioritisation or stop decision (retiring a feature or saying no).
  6. Developing a product manager, including a difficult feedback conversation.
- **Smart questions to ask:**
  - Which parts of the portfolio are being rationalised, and which are being rebuilt as AI-first?
  - How are autonomy levels and human review defined for AI features in this product?
  - How do you measure "value realised" versus "savings identified", and who signs off?
  - How is the roadmap split between platform, client-specific needs and compliance deadlines?
  - What does the India product team own end to end versus in partnership with US product leadership?
  - What do the first 90 days look like for a new Lead TPM?
